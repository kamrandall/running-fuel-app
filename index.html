      Product Notes

        Sweat-Rate Field Test

        Store a personal sweat baseline from a standardized 60-minute run.

        What counts as a standardized 60-minute run? Use a steady 60-minute outdoor or treadmill run at the kind of effort you want this calculator to represent, ideally close to your typical long-run or race effort in similar weather. Wear similar clothing, start normally hydrated, towel off before the final weigh-in, and avoid pausing for long breaks so the sweat-rate estimate reflects actual running conditions.

        Gut Training Progression

        Progress toward marathon-level intake without overwhelming the gut.

        How to rate GI symptom severity
        • None: no meaningful bloating, nausea, cramping, reflux, or urgency during or after the run.
        • Mild: noticeable but manageable symptoms that do not force pace changes or fueling changes.
        • Moderate: symptoms strong enough to disrupt rhythm, make fueling unpleasant, or create concern about finishing the session comfortably.
        • Severe: vomiting, severe cramping, urgent bathroom stops, dizziness linked to fueling, or symptoms bad enough that you slow sharply or stop the run.
